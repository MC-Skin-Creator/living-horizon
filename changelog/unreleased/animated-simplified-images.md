en: **Simplified images keep moving** — Distant mobs, players and entities drawn as simplified images now keep moving: they walk, ride with their mount's legs moving, and row their boats, instead of gliding along frozen in one pose. This uses more video memory: each one that moves takes three times as much for its images. *Animated Picture Distance* sets how far they keep moving, *Animated Pictures* turns it off, and *Picture Preview...* shows which ones are animated.
