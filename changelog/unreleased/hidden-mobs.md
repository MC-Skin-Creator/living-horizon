en: **Shareable list of hidden mobs** — `config/livinghorizon.json` now has a `hiddenMobs` list of the mobs never shown in the distance, by id (`"minecraft:pig"`) or for a whole mod (`"cobblemon:*"`). Modpacks can share their choices in one short list, and mobs set to *Hidden* in *Mobs & Entities...* are added to it.
